## PART A · THE PUBLIC WEBSITE (what visitors see)

### 1. What the website is for
For restaurants, bars and hotels in Thailand: the visitor books a free tasting of The Mexican Cola.

### 2. Who visits it
- First target: owners of Mexican and Latin restaurants in Thailand.
- Also: hotel bar managers (orders go through purchasing) and cocktail bar managers.
- Cities: Bangkok, Pattaya, Hua Hin, Koh Samui, Phuket.
- Where they come from: our cold calls, Instagram (@themexicancola), venue posters and table stands, and links we send on LINE and email. **[Proposal]** for the traffic sources. Notion does not list them.
- Most visitors come on a phone.

### 3. Pages
1. Home (the landing page, 11 blocks).
2. Thank you page (after the form).
3. What venues say.
4. Where to find us (all venues that bought, grouped by city).
5. Why us vs others.
6. Brand.
7. Founder story.
8. Admin login and admin pages (Part B).

### 4. Sections on each page
**Home**, top to bottom:

1. Top bar: logo, slogan "Happiness without Guilt", EN / TH switch, button.
2. Hero: tag "Built for the Thai hospitality industry". Headline: Your guests will ask: "What is this?" Photo: our bottle at Delia.
3. The problem: every menu has the same four colas.
4. How we help: three benefits with venue quotes (Sala Burger, Matti's, Cholos).
5. How it works: book a tasting, taste with your team, order from 1 case.
6. Proof: 26 venues, 95 cases sold, 10 venues reordered.
7. Who it is for: restaurants, bars and hotels. Not for the cheapest cola on the shelf.
8. The offer: free tasting, THB 1,320 a case of 24, THB 55 a bottle (THB 49 from 10 cases), free delivery, consignment, free posters and table stands.
9. Questions: six objections with answers.
10. Final call to action: promise, form, button.
11. Footer: contact, data line, note on where quotes come from.

**Thank you page:** "Thanks. We will contact you within 1 working day to book your tasting." Contact details. Link back home.

**Founder story**, top to bottom:

1. Intro: "Ansh, Founder of The Mexican Cola". Started at 18, with no background in drinks.
2. The story in 8 chapters, each with real photos: Before it all (ACK Sneakers, est. 2022, 300+ pairs sold). The spark (Nike SB x Jarritos, 10.05.2023). The search (100+ emails to Jarritos). The yes (sole distributor agreement for Thailand). The wait (Jarritos could not enter, so we made our own cola). Soleados (three flavours, close to 400 bottles sampled at 23 venues). 30 calls (about 30 calls and 4 meetings to win Cholos, now the bestseller in all 4 Cholos stores). Today (one hero product, now in restaurants, bars and hotels across Thailand).
3. Message to the next generation. Ends with: "If fortune favours the brave, then it also prefers the bold."
4. Timeline from 2022 to 11.2026.
5. Button: "Book a free tasting".

Why it is in: Cholos said they chose us "probably because of the founder". The founder story post (01.11.2026) is our most-liked Instagram post.

Rules: no Nike SB or Jarritos logos or product photos. They belong to other brands and could suggest a partnership. The story tells that part in words only.

**Other pages:** already built on the current venue site. Same top bar, footer and button.

### 5. Forms and what happens after
- One form, in block 10. Button text: "Book a free tasting" / "จองชิมฟรี", the same everywhere.
- Required fields: Name, Email, "What cola is on your menu today?". Email must be valid.
- Extra fields: Venue name, Phone or LINE ID, City (Bangkok, Pattaya, Hua Hin, Koh Samui, Phuket, Other), Preferred tasting date and time.
- The answer is saved in an enquiries table in a database, with all fields, date, time and language (EN or TH).
- The visitor sees the thank you page.
- The founder gets an email for each new enquiry at hello.themexicancola@gmail.com.

### 6. Text and content
- All text comes from Notion: Mandem HQ / Landing page / Copy (English and Thai).
- Founder story text, photos and timeline come from Notion: Knowledge base / "What is our founder story?".
- Photos: real photos only, listed in the Brand guide. No AI product photos.
- Only venues that bought appear. Sampled venues stay off.
- Thai text must be checked by a native speaker before launch.

## PART B · THE ADMIN SIDE (what only you see)

### 7. Who logs in
- Only the founder. **[Proposal]** one admin account, login with email and password at /admin.
- No public sign-up. Every admin page is closed to visitors who are not logged in.

### 8. What you see
- A list of all enquiries, newest first.
- Columns: date, name, venue, city, email, phone or LINE ID, current cola, preferred tasting date, language, status.
- Counts at the top: new this week, tastings booked, orders.
- Click a row to open the full enquiry with notes and its status history.

### 9. What you can do
- Change the status of an enquiry.
- Add a note (for example: "Tasting 20.11.2026, 15:00, bring 6 bottles").
- Search by name, venue or email. Filter by status and city.
- Export the list as a CSV file.

### 10. Statuses
New → Contacted → Tasting booked → Tasted → Ordered → Lost.

"Tasted" is its own step on purpose. Notion shows that 0 of 14 sampled venues have ordered. The follow-up after the tasting is our weak step, so we want to see it.

## PART C · FOR BOTH

### 11. Design
- Brand guide in Notion: Mandem HQ / Brand guide.
- Figma: [AI for startups] design, page "Design system": https://www.figma.com/design/aYDzSR1A9bUfLQo5KfG4eC?node-id=0-1

### 12. Must work on
- Phone (390 wide) first, then laptop (1440 wide).
- English and Thai, with the same layout. The slogan stays in English.
- Current Chrome and Safari, on iPhone and Android.

### 13. Not in this version
- Online ordering or payment.
- Customer accounts for venues.
- A blog.
- More than two languages.
- The class site pages (Problems, Target audience, Market size, Pitch deck). They hold business numbers.
- **[Proposal]** automatic reply emails to the visitor, and more than one admin user.
