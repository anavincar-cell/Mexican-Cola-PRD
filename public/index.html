    Founder story

    From sneakers to soda.

    How a Nike SB collaboration, 100 emails and one stubborn idea became The Mexican Cola, in one year.

    ส่วนนี้มีเฉพาะภาษาอังกฤษ

    01
    The spark

    It started with shoes.

    I have loved sneakers since I was young. At 14, I started my own business, ACK Sneakers, reselling sneakers, and I have now sold over 300 pairs. Then, on 10 May 2023, Nike SB released a collaboration with Jarritos, a Mexican soda brand. I was studying Spanish at school, and I thought: if it is on a Nike shoe, it must be something big. The bottle just looked so good. I had to try it.

    02
    The search

    No one in Thailand had it.

    I searched Mexican restaurants across Thailand. Not one of them served it. So I sent over 100 emails to Jarritos teams in Singapore, Australia and around the world.

    100+ emails. Almost no replies.

    03
    The yes

    One reply changed everything.

    For a long time, nothing came back. Then one day, the regional manager for Asia and the Middle East wrote to me. I got a gentleman's agreement to be the sole distributor of Jarritos in Thailand.

    Sole distributor of Jarritos in Thailand, on a handshake.

    04
    The wait

    What if they never come?

    Jarritos could not get into the country yet. After a couple of months of waiting, I asked myself what would happen if they never arrived. Instead of waiting, I decided to make our own.

    I had no background in drinks or beverages at all. I started anyway, and I learnt. I got scammed, and I learnt more. I failed, got back up, and just kept going.

    No background. Got scammed. Failed. Kept going.

    05
    Soleados

    Three flavours, 400 bottles, 23 venues.

    We started as Soleados, which means “full of sun”, a name about happiness. We had three flavours: tepache (fermented pineapple), pandan coconut with a Mexican twist, and Mexican cola. We sampled close to 400 bottles across 23 venues in Thailand.

    TepachePandan coconutMexican cola

    On the road

    06
    One hero

    One flavour stood out.

    It was the Mexican cola. Soleados was hard for local guests to connect with, so we chose a name that says what it is: The Mexican Cola. Instead of many flavours, we perfected one. Rich with herbs and spices, 0% caffeine, 100% natural sugar.

    07
    30 calls

    Persistence opened the door.

    I cold-called Cholos about 30 times. I went to 4 meetings before we got to sit down with the owner. Only then did we get a trial run. Today, we are the bestseller in all 4 of their stores, with a fifth opening in Phuket in December 2026.

    30 cold calls. 4 meetings. 1 trial run. Now the bestseller in every Cholos store.

    08
    Today

    Proving itself, one venue at a time.

    It is a bestseller in Mexican venues, and now it is going into bars, hotels and restaurants across Thailand. It has been a tough journey, and growing will be tougher. We will fight until we succeed.

    The cost of being disciplined: every day stays the same, but every year is different. The cost of being undisciplined: every day is different, but every year is the same.

    Ansh, Founder of Mexican Cola

    A message to the next generation

    It was never a straight line.

    I was 18 when I started The Mexican Cola, with no background in drinks. Reading this, you might think it was a straight line. It wasn't. I got scammed. I got rejected at venues. My stomach filled with butterflies before every meeting. I called Cholos about 30 times before they gave us a chance.

    Every one of those challenges and failures taught me something. You will fail. Learn to fail forward. You will pivot many times. For a while, you will be your biggest supporter, because this path is lonely.

    So if you are fighting to start a business, or to keep one alive, the worst thing you can do is give up. Keep moving. Keep fighting for your chance. If you don't give up, it will come one way or another.

    If fortune favours the brave, then it also prefers the bold.

    Ansh, Founder of Mexican Cola
